# SOC Analyst Training Program — Complete Course Index

**Target Audience:** Complete beginners with zero technical background
**Goal:** Job-ready SOC Tier 1 Analyst (with Tier 2 foundations)
**Format:** Self-paced digital training with exercises, log labs, and scenarios

---

## How to Use This Course

Read every lesson in order. Do not skip phases.
Every concept builds on the one before it.
Complete every exercise before moving to the next lesson.
Take every quiz before moving to the next phase.
